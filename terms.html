4. Return & Refund Policy

In full compliance with Section 56 of the South African Consumer Protection Act (CPA No. 68 of 2008), we stand behind the quality and integrity of all materials supplied:

Damaged, Defective or Misprinted Physical Books

If your physical book arrives damaged, with missing pages, or misprints:

  1. Notify us within 3 calendar days of delivery by emailing photos of the defect and your order reference to eliza.reconnection@gmail.com.
  2. Return the damaged product and we will immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement copy free of charge, or issue a full refund of the purchase price.

Personalized & Author Inscribed Books

Where an author dedication or custom inscription was specifically requested by the customer, such items represent bespoke, personalized goods under Section 42(2)(f) of the ECTA. They cannot be returned for a change-of-mind, but remain fully guaranteed against any physical defect or courier damage.

Digital Publications (PDF Downloads)

Due to the immediate access nature of electronic downloads, digital publications are non-refundable once downloaded. If you experience technical difficulty opening the PDF file, we will promptly re-issue a working copy to your email address or assist with alternate access.

Refund Processing Method: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original funding card, bank account, or digital wallet through the PayFast Merchant Gateway. Processing typically completes within 5 to 7 South African banking days.
